Effective date: November 4, 2026 · Adults only (18+)

In short

NaberX is a dating app for adults aged 18 and over. It shows you other NaberX users who are physically close to you, over Bluetooth only. If you both like each other, you match and can chat. Your messages never go over the internet, there are no servers and no accounts, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data. Seeing who is nearby is free; liking people and matching needs a paid Dating Pass, bought through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The developer cannot see your messages and does not check or control them. You alone are responsible for everything you write and send, and for how you meet people.

The App is only for people aged 18 or older. When you first open the App, you must confirm that you are 18 or older; if you are not, you must not use the App. Your confirmation is stored only on your phone and is never shared with anyone. Giving a false answer about your age is a breach of these terms. We do not verify anyone's identity, age or background, and we do not screen users.

3. Data we do not collect

NaberX does not send your messages or any personal data over the internet, and we do not run any server. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share your messages, matches, nickname, bio, location or other personal information. There are no user accounts, no phone number or email sign-up, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. The only exception is the limited order information Google Play shares with us when you buy a Dating Pass (see section 11).

4. Data stored only on your phone

To work, the App keeps the following on your own device: the nickname and short bio you choose, a random identifier created for this installation, your matches, your messages, the people you unmatched or blocked, your age confirmation, your subscription status and your settings. This data stays in the App's private storage on your phone and is saved in encrypted form. It is not backed up to us and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes it permanently (an active subscription can be restored from Google Play, see section 11).

5. What nearby people can see

While you are visible, NaberX users within Bluetooth range can see your nickname, your short bio, roughly how close you are, and the random identifier of this installation. You do not need to use your real name. Liking someone tells them only that you liked them. People you match with receive the messages you send them, and those messages are then stored on their phone, outside our and your control. Using Unmatch asks the other phone to delete the match and the chat too, but this cannot be guaranteed (for example, someone may take a screenshot). Do not send anything you would not want the other person to keep.

6. Bluetooth and permissions

The App asks for Bluetooth permissions (scan, advertise and connect) only to find nearby NaberX users and exchange messages with them. On some older Android versions, the system requires location permission for Bluetooth scanning; the App does not read, store or share your location. To sell subscriptions, the App uses Google Play's billing service, which talks to the Google Play Store app on your phone. You can turn invisible mode on at any time to stop Bluetooth completely, and you can revoke permissions in your phone's settings.

7. Encryption and security

Messages travel directly from phone to phone over Bluetooth and are end-to-end encrypted. Matches are not verified with a code, so the App cannot prove who is behind a nickname. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that data on your device or another person's device will never be accessed, lost or disclosed (for example, if a phone is lost, unlocked or compromised).
